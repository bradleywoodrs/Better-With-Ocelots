# BetterWithOcelots

This mod adds ocelots and cats into BTA!

- Ocelots only spawn in rainforest biomes, and can’t spawn below y 63.
- Sneaking up to an ocelot while holding a raw fish has a chance to tempt it closer, and right clicking it with a raw fish has a chance to tame it into a cat.
- Right clicking a tamed cat will make it sit down at that loc.
- Right clicking a tamed cat with a fish will heal that cat if hurt, or put it into breed mode if it’s at full health.
- Two tamed cats can breed and will spawn a kitten, which grows into an adult after 20 minutes.
- Tamed cats will wander around while the owner is within 32 blocks, otherwise they will stay put. If a tamed cat can’t find a path it will teleport to the owner.
- Tamed cats occasionally turn their heads to look at a player.
- Tamed cats will occasionally run to, jump, and sit on furniture, and will stay there for 1-3 minutes. 
- Furniture blocks cats will sit on as long as there is air above it: Chests, Active Furnaces, Beds, and Seats
- Kittens will occasionally jump onto their owner’s shoulder and sit there for 1-3 minutes. Press shift to make them jump down.
- Ocelots/cats will avoid water unless following their owner or being tempted by a fish.
- Ocelots/cats will avoid lava, walk around fences, and won’t climb on fences or trap doors.
- Ocelots will pounce on and attack chickens.
- Creepers will run away from ocelots/cats unless they’re already about to explode.
- Right clicking a tamed cat while sitting in a boat will make the cat sit in the boat with you, if a kitten is on your shoulder when getting into a boat, the kitten will sit in the boat.
- 11 Variants of cats, 8 from modern mc.
- Fun Holiday Features for Halloween and Christmas.
- Youthful Flower Blend when given to a cow has a chance to turn the cow into a youthful cow.
- Using a bucket on a youthful cow will give you a Bucket of Milk of Eternal Youth.
- Using a Bucket of Milk of Eternal Youth on a kitten will allow that kitten to stay a kitten forever regardless of config setting.
